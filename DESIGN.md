# Gravebound Pinball: the rules we settled on

Pinball first. Flippers and a nudge are the only inputs; everything else is done by hitting the table.
Good pinball play wins the run. Everything below adds spectacle, forgiveness and scoring on top of that.

## The hero

You pick a class for your ball: Knight, Rogue, Mage or Cleric. A class has three tiers of power plus the party:

| Tier | Trigger | What it is |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Nudge power | Nudge, short cooldown | Shockwave, Shadowstep, Spark, Ward Pulse |
| Cradle shot | One bar of the meter, cradle 0.8 s | Charge, Backstab, Fireball, Consecrate: the next flip arms the shot |
| Spellbook | Table goals | Four timed abilities per class, unlocked and upgraded on level-up, cast by shots |
| Rally | Three bars, cradle 1.5 s | The party comes out as balls for a limited time |

## The spellbook

Every class has four abilities, one per purpose. Each is a timed power that rides the ball.

| Purpose | Knight | Rogue | Mage | Cleric |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Breaker, clears what blocks a shot | Charge | Cloak | Frost Nova | Consecration |
| Guard, keeps the ball alive | Aegis | Smoke | Arcane Barrier | Sanctuary |
| Striker, boss and wave damage | Warcry | Marked Prey | Meteor Shower | Judgement |
| Pressure, thins waves | Shield Wall | Fan of Knives | Blink Strike | Radiance |

- **Unlocking is the level-up.** The level-up screen shows the same four cards every time. Locked: Unlock.
  Unlocked: its rank and Level Up, which opens that ability's three upgrade rows (Duration is always one)
  with concrete numbers and a Back. One pause, one reward per level. Level-ups wait for a scoop, lock or
  ball end.
- **Casting is earned on the table.** Each purpose has a goal on every terrace: Breaker completes a bank,
  Guard rolls three lanes then shoots a scoop, Striker makes two ramps, Pressure makes two orbits or twenty
  spins. Unlocking lights the goal; completing it casts the ability for its duration and resets the goal.
  All unlocked abilities have their goals live at once, so a lucky or skilled player stacks them. Recasting
  one that is running extends its timer, up to twice the base duration.
- The meter's bars are for the cradle shot (one) and the Rally (three). What two bars do is open.

Table boons (Ramp Runner, Second Chance, Tollgate...), plain stats and the rare keystones stay in the
game. They are drawn after each boss kill, one pick, where play is already paused. A ritual that hands
them out from a lit scoop is a later pass.

## The party

- Companions are classes or roles, never named people, never a story. For now: Tank, Healer, Striker.
- You pick one with your hero, free, or ride alone. That is the only choice before the plunger.
- The companion stands guard at the bottom drain on the Hollow, between the flippers, before the Grave.
  A draining ball bounces off them at a cost to their health; a ball off the post fires their perk; swarms go
  for them; at zero they are wounded and the drain stands open until a bank is completed.
- The Tavern offers three random recruits a visit, each a class plus one of its abilities ("Rogue, Fan of Knives"),
  shown as the ball's portrait, the class, the ability and one line. It opens only when you can pay and have a seat
  (six). Recruits come out in Rally as balls of their class with that ability running.

## Rally

- Cradle three bars for 1.5 s (a ring fills, so it is always deliberate). Party balls spawn beside the hero,
  one per party member, for 20 s, each playing its class or role. They vanish when the time ends or if
  they drain. No penalty for losing them.
- The hero ball is the one that matters: the camera follows it, and draining it ends the Rally and counts as
  a lost ball, as always. A 3 s ball save covers the launch.
- Party balls score and kill normally but do not collect jackpots. The real multiball from the locks is a
  separate system with its own rules.
- Big is fine. Five balls, twenty balls: mash the flippers, lose most of them, reign it in.

## Foes

Every foe has a job on the table, a visible wind-up, and a shot that answers it (see README). Waves come
with a lull after; shots call, feed or hold them. Bosses use the table. Enemies exist to block shots,
threaten the ball and gate progress; abilities exist to answer that.

## Fixed points

- The Grave: an open Grave always takes the ball, tilted, fallen or not. Tilt forfeits the ball bonus only.
- A run is one campaign; winning ends it with a summary. Endless mode chains campaigns.
- Keystones (rare boons) stay.
- More tables with other themes come after the rule set is solid.
